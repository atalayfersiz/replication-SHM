Prototype Validation & Methodology

Framework Evaluation (Hielscher et al., 2023)

This prototype implements and evaluates the core data-processing pipeline of the paper "A neural network based digital twin model for the structural health monitoring of reinforced concrete bridges":

  • Baseline Thermal Strains: Temperature sensors are used to predict the baseline strains at all 64 sensor locations using an Artificial Neural Network (MLP) and linear baselines.
  • Load Strain Extraction: Live mechanical strains are isolated by subtracting the predicted baseline thermal strains from the total raw sensor measurements.
  • Structural Calculations: Curvature is calculated from top/bottom sensor pairs, fitted with a polynomial, and double-integrated to calculate vertical deflections.

Simulation & Assumptions

Since the field dataset from the Sydney footbridge is proprietary, the inputs have been simulated using physically-informed parameters:

  • Bridge Geometry: 17.9m span length with a supporting pier column at 8.95m.
  • Sensor Layout: 32 longitudinal positions containing top and bottom strain arrays, and 4 temperature sensors.
  • Moving Load: A 1.1-tonne pallet jack moving across the bridge deck.
  • Material Properties & Boundary Conditions: Concrete Young's Modulus E = 38 GPa, moment of inertia I = 0.00475 m&sup4;. Deflections are double-integrated using continuous beam boundary conditions (zero deflection at $x = 0$ m, $x = 8.95$ m, and $x = 17.9$ m).

Sensor Configuration Sensitivity Study (t = 13 min / Load at 14m)

To evaluate the framework under different sensor densities, we tested three configurations. The comparative error metrics show how spatial density affects structural reconstruction under continuous support constraints:

Sensor Setup Poly Degree Peak Moment Moment MAE Peak Deflection Deflection MAE
Full (16 sensors) 4 6.72 kNm 1.05 kNm -0.23 mm 0.054 mm
Reduced (8 sensors) 4 10.01 kNm 1.15 kNm -0.23 mm 0.050 mm
Sparse (4 sensors) 2 15.26 kNm 3.32 kNm -0.36 mm 0.105 mm

Metrics are computed against the paper's reference DT bending moment (Peak: 7.56 kNm) and FE deflection (Peak: -0.22 mm). Piecewise curvature integration is applied to satisfy zero deflection at the midspan pier support. Note that the 4-sensor setup limits polynomial fitting to degree 2 (quadratic), which cannot capture the double curvature over the midspan column, leading to massive errors.
